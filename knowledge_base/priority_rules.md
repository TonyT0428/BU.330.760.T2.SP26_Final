## Priority rules

Use these guidelines to set `priority`:

- **High**
  - Hard deadline explicitly stated (date) or “today / EOD / ASAP”
  - Blocking another team or a release
  - Customer-impacting issue or incident
- **Medium**
  - Important work without strong urgency cues
  - Planned sprint work, follow-ups, coordination tasks
- **Low**
  - Nice-to-have improvements
  - Vague “keep in mind” items
  - Optional exploration / research

If you are unsure, choose **Medium** and lower `confidence`.
