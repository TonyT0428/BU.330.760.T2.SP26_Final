## Assignee conventions

- If a person explicitly volunteers (“I’ll take X”) or is assigned (“Alex to do X”), use that name.
- If a team is mentioned (“mobile team”), keep the assignee as `"Unassigned"` unless a specific
  person is named.
- If the notes use pronouns without a clear referent, set assignee `"Unassigned"`.
- Do not guess based on job titles.
