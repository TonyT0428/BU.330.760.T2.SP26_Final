## Action item style guide

When writing `task`:

- Start with a verb (imperative): “Draft…”, “Schedule…”, “Send…”, “Fix…”.
- Keep it specific and testable.
- Split compound tasks only if they are clearly separable.
- Exclude decisions and discussion notes unless they imply a follow-up task.

Examples:
- Good: “Email the vendor to confirm pricing”
- Bad: “Pricing was discussed”
